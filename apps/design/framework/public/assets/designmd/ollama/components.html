Frequently asked questions

Is Ollama free?
Yes — running models locally is completely free. Pro and Max add hosted cloud inference for teams that need scale.
Which models are supported?
Llama, Mistral, Gemma, Phi, and hundreds more from the model library — plus your own custom GGUF files.
Does it work offline?
Once a model is downloaded, everything runs on your machine with no internet connection required.
How do I get support?
Community help is available on Discord and GitHub. Pro and Max plans include priority support.
