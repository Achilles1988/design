How is Fin priced?

Fin is billed per resolution — you only pay $0.99 when Fin successfully resolves a customer conversation, so cost scales with outcomes.

Can I use Intercom without Fin?

Yes. The help desk, inbox, and reporting work fully on their own. Fin is an add-on you can enable whenever you're ready.

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial — no credit card required. You can invite your team and connect channels in minutes.
